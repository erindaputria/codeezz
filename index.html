The Story

⚔️

The Dance of the Dragons

A civil war between two Targaryen factions — the Blacks who supported Rhaenyra Targaryen and the Greens who supported Aegon II. This conflict became the turning point in the downfall of the Targaryen dynasty.

👑

The Iron Throne

After the death of King Viserys I, a fierce struggle for the throne erupted, leading to all-out war. The ancient oaths sworn by lords to Rhaenyra were ignored, sparking bloodshed within the family.

🔥

Fire and Blood

Mighty dragons from both sides clashed in epic battles across the skies of Westeros. Yet as more dragons perished, the power of House Targaryen weakened further across the world.
